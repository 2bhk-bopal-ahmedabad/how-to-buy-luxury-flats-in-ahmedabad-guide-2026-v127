Legal Considerations When Purchasing Luxury Flats

Understanding the legalities involved in buying luxury flats is vital. You should consider the following aspects:


    Title Deed: Verify the property’s title deed to ensure there are no encumbrances.
    Land Use Approval: Ensure the property complies with zoning laws and land use approvals.
    Occupancy Certificate: Confirm the flat has an occupancy certificate, validating its compliance with building regulations.
    Property Tax: Review any outstanding property taxes that could affect your purchase.
    Sale Agreement: Examine the sale agreement meticulously to understand all terms and conditions.
    Legal Representation: Hiring a legal advisor familiar with real estate transactions can safeguard your investment.
